The 20-second explanation

Entity resolution is the art of knowing
when “different” records are the same entity.

An entity can be a person, a business or another real-world thing. Entity resolution compares clues across records to work out which ones belong together—even when names or details vary. U1 connects those records into a single profile that your teams can use.

Think of it as introducing your systems to the customer they already share.

Good questions

Let’s clear up a few things.

Is this just removing duplicate records?

Deduplication helps tidy a dataset. Entity resolution goes further: it connects records about the same entity across datasets, bringing different pieces of the relationship into a shared view.

Do we need to replace our existing systems?

A shared profile can bring context from existing systems together. We start by assessing your data sources and workflows, then agree the integration approach that fits your environment.

What happens when the data disagrees?

Conflicting addresses, shared phone numbers and old details need careful handling. Part of designing your U1 solution is agreeing which sources to trust, what counts as a match and when a case needs review.

Where should we start?

Choose one business problem and the datasets behind it. For example: helping support recognise customers across CRM and billing. Agree how to measure improvement, validate the results, then expand from that foundation.
